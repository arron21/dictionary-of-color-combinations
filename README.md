# 配色総鑑 — A Dictionary of Color Combinations (Vol. 1 & Vol. 2)

An interactive digital archive of **Sanzo Wada’s (和田三造, 1883–1967)** iconic Japanese color theory books: ***A Dictionary of Color Combinations, Volume 1 & Volume 2***.

## Features

- **Complete Volume 1 & Volume 2 Archive (`450` Combinations):**
  - **Volume 1 (*Haishoku Soukan*, 1933):** 360 classic 2-, 3-, and 4-color combinations.
  - **Volume 2 (*Haishoku Soukan B-hen & Shinsaku Zuanshu*, 1935–1937):** 72 seasonal palettes organized across the 12 months of the Japanese calendar (`Mutsuki` through `Shiwasu`) + 18 applied design motifs (*Kimono*, *Clothes & Accessories*, *Tapestry & Carpets*, *Earthenware & Lacquerware*, *Book, Poster & Wrapping Paper*).
- **171 Traditional & Modern Pigments:**
  - Includes original English (Ridgway) nomenclature, traditional Japanese Kanji & Romaji names, pigment meanings, `HEX`, `RGB`, `CMYK`, `HSL`, and `CIE LAB` values.
- **One-Click Copy & Multi-Format Export:**
  - Click any swatch bar or hex chip to copy its code (`HEX`, `RGB`, `CMYK`, `HSL`, or `CSS Variable`).
  - Export whole combinations as HEX lists, CSS Custom Properties, Tailwind configs, JSON objects, or downloadable **SVG Swatch Cards**.
- **Applied Harmony & WCAG 2.1 Contrast Inspector:**
  - Inspect pairwise contrast ratios (`AA` / `AAA`) and preview combinations with rotatable color roles.
